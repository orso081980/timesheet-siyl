SIYL – Find Professionals Who Speak Your Language What is SIYL? SIYL is a platform that connects international residents, expats, and non-Italian speakers in Sardinia with local professionals who can communicate in their language. From accountants to real estate agents to veterinarians, users can easily find, trust, and book professionals without language barriers. The Problem Thousands of people living in Sardinia struggle to: Find reliable professionals Communicate clearly due to language barriers Trust unknown service providers Navigate fragmented information (Facebook groups, word of mouth) At the same time, many local professionals: Miss opportunities with international clients Lack visibility in the expat market Face communication challenges The Solution SIYL solves this by offering a language-first platform where users can: 🔍 Search professionals by language, profession, and location ✅ Access verified language skills ⭐ View reviews and profiles 📅 Book directly through the platform For professionals, SIYL provides: Increased visibility to international clients Qualified leads (not random inquiries) A simple way to showcase language capabilities How It Works For Users Search by language + service needed Browse verified professionals Book directly or contact instantly For Professionals Create a profile Get language skills verified Receive visibility + booking requests Key Differentiation Unlike traditional directories, SIYL is: 🌍 Language-first (not just profession-based) 🤝 Trust-focused (verification + reviews) ⚡ Action-oriented (direct booking, not just discovery) 🎯 Niche-focused (expat & international market) Why It Matters Global mobility is increasing, and regions like Sardinia are attracting more: Expats Remote workers International families However, the infrastructure to support them is still fragmented. SIYL bridges this gap — improving access, communication, and trust. Partnership Opportunities We are looking to collaborate with: Local organizations & communities Professional networks Tourism & relocation services Coworking spaces and expat groups Partnerships can include: Visibility & cross-promotion Access to international audiences Co-creation of trusted local networks Vision Start in Sardinia → expand across Italy → scale to other countries with similar challenges.
